# Storyboard — Decimal Forms: From Fractions to Terminating & Recurring Decimals

**Chapter:** Chapter 1 — Rational Numbers
**Concept:** Rational numbers as terminating or recurring decimals via division.
**Target duration:** 2:20–2:40
**Core visual idea:** Fraction -> Long Division -> Decimal Pattern.

## Scene flow
1. Intro Card — 5/8, 3/7 preview, p/q -> ?
2. A Fraction Is a Division — p/q = p÷q.
3. Terminating Decimal — 5÷8 = 0.625, remainders 2,4,0, STOP.
4. Recurring Decimal — 3÷7 = 0.428571 repeating, remainders 3,2,6,4,5,1 -> back to 3, LOOP.
5. Why Do Digits Repeat? — remainder cycle diagram.
6. Side-by-Side Comparison — STOP vs LOOP.
7. Your Turn — predict 3/4 (terminating) and 2/7 (recurring).
8. Summary — Divide -> Watch the remainder -> STOP or LOOP.

## Production rule
Animate the division progressively (digit by digit with remainder shown), not all at once. Same remainder reappearing is the visual/causal reason digits repeat.
