# Storyboard: The Balancing Scales of Transposition

**Topic:** Linear Equations in One Variable (Chapter 2, Telangana Class VIII Mathematics)  
**Target Duration:** ~90 Seconds  
**Format:** Story-Mode Chalkboard Lesson (VoiceoverScene + edge-tts + burned-in captions)

---

## Characters & Key Motifs

1. **x (The Mystery Box):** Friendly crate with detective hat holding a hidden number.
2. **The Seesaw Fulcrum (= Sign):** Animated equal sign acting as the pivot of a balanced seesaw.
3. **+5 Weight:** Heavy trapezoidal iron weight on the Left-Hand Side.
4. **-5 Balloon:** Lighter-than-air balloon created when +5 flips as it crosses the = sign.
5. **Stack of 20 Coins:** Balance weight on the Right-Hand Side.
6. **x 3 Badge & Division Bar:** Multiplier 3 nudged across the fulcrum, dropping below the fraction line to divide 15.

---

## Storyboard Timeline & Beats

### Beat 0: Title
- Header: *The Balancing Scales of Transposition*
- Subtitle: *Solving 3x + 5 = 20*

### Beat 1: The Balanced Seesaw (0:00 – 0:20)
- Equation:  + 5 = 20$
- Visuals: Balanced seesaw with glowing = pivot. LHS has 3 Mystery Boxes (3x) and a +5 weight. RHS has 20 coins.
- Dialogue:
  - Narrator: 'An equation is just like a balanced seesaw! Whatever sits on the Left-Hand Side equals whatever sits on the Right-Hand Side. Our goal? Uncover the mystery number inside x!'
  - x (Mystery Box): 'Help me get isolated so I can reveal my true value!'

### Beat 2: Crossing the Pivot — Addition to Subtraction (0:20 – 0:45)
- Rule 1: $+a \longrightarrow -a$.
- Visuals: +5 slides toward the = pivot, sparks fly, it turns upside down into a -5 balloon floating to the RHS.  - 5 = 15$. Equation updates to  = 15$.
- Dialogue:
  - Narrator: 'Rule Number 1: When a term crosses the equal sign pivot, it flips its sign! +5 on the left becomes -5 on the right.'
  - +5 Weight: 'Whoosh! Addition becomes Subtraction!'
  - Narrator: 'Now we have  = 15$, and the seesaw stays perfectly balanced!'

### Beat 3: Crossing the Line — Multiplication to Division (0:45 – 1:10)
- Rule 2: $\times a \longrightarrow \div a$.
- Visuals: $ nudges the $\times 3$ badge toward the pivot. As it crosses, it drops below a horizontal fraction line under 15, becoming the denominator $\frac{15}{3}$.  \div 3 = 5$. Equation updates to  = 5$.
- Dialogue:
  - Narrator: 'Rule Number 2: A multiplier on one side drops below the line to become a divisor on the other side!'
  - $\times 3$ Badge: 'Down I go! Multiplication turns into Division!'
  - Narrator: '15 divided by 3 gives us 5!'

### Beat 4: Verification & The Final Takeaway (1:10 – 1:30)
- Visuals: $ pops open with confetti, revealing 5 inside! Verification scale balances (5) + 5 = 15 + 5 = 20$.
- Dialogue:
  - $ Box: 'I am 5! Let\'s check:  \times 5 + 5 = 20$. It balances!'
  - Narrator: 'Transposition is simply reversing operations! Addition switches with Subtraction, and Multiplication switches with Division. Master the pivot, and you can solve any linear equation!'

### Beat 5: Reversal Rules Summary Table & Outro
- Summary Table:
  - $+ \longleftrightarrow -$
  - $\times \longleftrightarrow \div$
- Thank You for Watching!
