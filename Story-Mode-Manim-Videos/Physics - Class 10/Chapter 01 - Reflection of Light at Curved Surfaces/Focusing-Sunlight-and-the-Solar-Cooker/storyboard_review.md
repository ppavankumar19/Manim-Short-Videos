# Storyboard review: Focusing Sunlight & the Solar Cooker

**Verdict: sound and ready to build.** The physics is correct, the sequence is
well ordered (hook, why parallel, the rule, the visible focus, why it heats, the
device, alignment, placement, quiz, recap) and the safeguards are the right
ones. Items worth knowing about, and what was done:

## Kept as written
- The wide-to-small "sunlight funnel" as the single visual idea; one fixed
  picture (Sun left, five parallel rays, dish right, F on the axis) used in
  every scene.
- The finding-the-focus activity (smallest, brightest spot) before naming F.
- The explicit "the mirror does not create energy" line.
- Source notes: scene 8 (vessel before / at / beyond F) is a model-created
  illustration, not a textbook statement; the video words it as a model.

## Physics points handled (not errors in the storyboard, but easy to get wrong)
1. **Exactness of "parallel rays meet at F".** A spherical mirror only does this
   for rays near the axis. So the video uses the textbook's spherical mirror
   (with P, F, C) only for the rule and the screen activity (scenes 3-4, narrow
   bundle), and a **parabolic dish** for the cooker scenes, where the reflected
   rays meet at F exactly. Real solar cookers are parabolic too.
2. **Scene 7 (turning the cooker).** Off-axis rays are reflected with the true
   law of reflection at the turned dish (not faked), so the bright region really
   leaves the vessel. In reality it becomes a smeared patch, not a point.
3. **Scene 4 spot size.** The patch on the screen is drawn from the true width
   of the reflected bundle at that position, so it really shrinks to a minimum
   at F. The Sun's image is a tiny disc, not a mathematical point, which is why
   the words are "smallest, brightest spot".
4. **Scene 2.** Added the real number (the Sun is about 150 million km away) so
   "far enough to be parallel" is concrete.

## Changes made to the storyboard
- **Safety line added to scene 4** (on screen and in the narration): focused
  sunlight can burn paper, shown as an animation only, never without a teacher.
  The storyboard asked for animation; this makes the warning explicit.
- **Position labels in scene 8** are "A, B, C" on the picture and "before F / at
  F / beyond F" in the cards. The storyboard's "C" clashes with the centre of
  curvature C used in scene 3, so the cards carry the meaning.
- **Quiz answers** shortened to fit the row ("THROUGH F" for challenge A).
- **Ten-second THINK clock** (the series rule) replaces the 4-second pauses;
  the clock is the small top-right ring, the questions stay fully visible.
- The qualitative **heat meter** is a horizontal bar under the diagram (no
  numbers, as the storyboard asks).

## Not done on purpose
- Cooker construction (frame, mirror pieces) is left out, as the storyboard's
  own safeguard F says.
